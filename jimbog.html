Stage 01

Drop Detection & Energy Mapping

The track is analyzed for drops, builds, and dynamics — visualized as an energy map that drives the edit before a single frame is generated.

Stage 02

Shot-by-Shot Prompt Generation

Full scripts broken into individual shots with narrative and performance cuts, timed to the beat map.

Stage 03

Style DNA Extraction

A consistent visual identity extracted and enforced across every shot, so the video reads as one world — not a slideshow of AI clips.

Stage 04

Character Anchor Locking

Multi-character anchor images lock faces and wardrobe across generations. Face drift — the #1 tell of AI video — solved at the pipeline level.

Stage 05

Dialect Translation

Every prompt automatically translated into the native "dialect" of each generator — Grok Imagine, Runway, Kling, Pika — for maximum fidelity per platform.

Stage 06

Edit & Finish

Assembled and finished in Adobe Premiere with color, pacing, and connective filler shots designed in deliberate temperature pairs.

Liner Notes

The Story

Jimbo G — Jimmie D. Gammon Jr. — has spent over twenty years inside music and entertainment, including two decades behind the counter at Karma Records, where the underground wasn't a genre. It was the day job.

He plays multiple instruments, writes constantly, draws, and is covered in tattoos that tell most of the story before he says a word. His range runs from chopper rap to doom R&B to worship — because the song comes first and the lane comes never.

Now he works at the collision point of that analog history and the AI frontier: building custom production tools, engineering AI video pipelines pulling 50k+ views per video for client bands, running SEO-driven client websites, and studying for a computer science degree — proving one independent artist with deep curiosity can run what used to take a full crew.

He's a father of two. That's the reason for all of it.
